### Original films, sound and captions

All seven supplied clips play in full. Their aspect ratios are preserved, including the portrait-format Session 3 clip. Their original speakers and soundtrack remain in place; sound levels have been balanced with the simulated facilitator using gain adjustments, without changing the spoken content or timing.

The facilitator narration has optional English captions in the MP4s, plus SRT and VTT sidecars. The player provides a Captions button. These captions follow the narration around the original films and activity pauses.

Six original films have captions already embedded in their pictures. Sampled frames were checked, but this does not certify every original caption’s accuracy or complete coverage. Caption size and style vary between those films.

The sensory-overwhelm animation in Session 2 has no embedded captions. A full local transcription pass detected no intelligible speech and returned only unverified music/sound-effect labels. Those labels have not been presented as accurate captions. Descriptive soundtrack captions would require a listening review. Its introduction correctly identifies it as an animation, and a quiet moment follows it.

### Checks performed

All 165 final narration files were independently transcribed locally and compared with the scripts. Two ambiguous phrases were regenerated and rechecked. Caption text was checked against the final scripts and aligned to the actual audio, with no captions extending into added working pauses or original films. Slide images, chapters, audio alignment and media format were also checked. Sound was balanced to approximately −21 LUFS with peak headroom.

These are source, technical and automated wording checks, supported by visual sampling; they are not a claim that a person listened to every minute. The selected voice was confirmed by Craig using the earlier voice sample. Retain the source gaps and original-film caption limitations when using this as a rehearsal resource.
